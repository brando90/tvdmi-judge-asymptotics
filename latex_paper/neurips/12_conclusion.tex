\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Separating \emph{statistical convergence} from \emph{semantic validity} explains when reference-free TVD-MI ensembles work and when naive pipelines fail:
(1)~double-centered oriented peer contrasts neutralize prompt copying and global inversions;
(2)~split-sample cross-fitting eliminates null winner's-curse inflation and sets compute-optimal $K^\star(B)$;
(3)~\texttt{RB-Spec TVD-MI} restores $1/F$ family scaling and clone/polarity robustness across \textsc{SummEval}, \textsc{MT-Bench}, and \textsc{RewardBench~2}; and
(4)~$5$--$20$ anchors close the $\mathrm{ICC(A,1)}$--$\mathrm{ICC(C,1)}$ gap while \texttt{CR-RB-Spec} removes measured confounders under Davis--Kahan bounds.

\textbf{Limitations.} Unmeasured shared confounders $W$ omitted from covariates require anchors; continuous measure-theoretic and full EM fixed-point rates remain open.
