\section{Lean~4 Formalization}
\label{sec:lean}

We formally verify all algebraic, finite-alphabet variational, invariance,
concentration-scaling, and optimality results in Lean~4 (\texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.29.0})
with Mathlib \citep{mathlib2020} across two modules in
\texttt{experiments/00\_lean\_formalization/lean/TvdmiJudge/}:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Variational duality and injection blindness} (\texttt{NewTheory.lean},
  Theorem~\ref{thm:injection-blindness}): Finite-alphabet TVD variational upper
  bound (\texttt{finite\_tvd\_variational\_le}), Scheff\'e attainment
  (\texttt{finite\_tvd\_variational\_eq}), and exact injection blindness
  $\tvd = 1 - \sum_x P_X(x)^2$ for arbitrary injective maps
  $f:\alpha \to \beta$ (\texttt{injection\_tvd\_blindness}).
\item \textbf{Double-centering invariance and oriented peer recovery}
  (\texttt{NewTheory.lean}, Theorem~\ref{thm:double-centering}): Exact
  cancellation of additive prompt/verbosity shifts $a(x) + b(y)$ inside the
  TVD-MI witness contrast (\texttt{tvdmi\_double\_centering\_invariance}), plus
  strict monotonicity and negative inversion rejection of the oriented peer
  formula (\texttt{oriented\_peer\_strict\_mono}, \texttt{oriented\_peer\_rejects\_inverter}).
\item \textbf{Equicorrelated variance, clone hijacking, and $1/F$ recovery}
  (\texttt{Basic.lean} and \texttt{NewTheory.lean}): Equicorrelated variance
  identities and persistent floor (\texttt{variance\_equicorrelated\_mean},
  \texttt{variance\_floor}), concrete clone-hijacking strict variance-increase
  counterexample (\texttt{clone\_hijacking\_strict\_increase\_counterexample}),
  asymptote to $\tau_1^2$ (\texttt{clone\_hijacking\_tendsto}), and uniform
  $B/F$ upper bound independent of clone counts (\texttt{var\_family\_balanced\_le\_inv\_F},
  \texttt{inv\_F\_rate\_vanishes}).
\item \textbf{Two-axis Hoeffding correction and split-sample convergence}
  (\texttt{NewTheory.lean}, Theorem~\ref{thm:two-axis}--\ref{thm:split-sample}):
  Exact two-sample Hoeffding exponent reduction to $n\eta^2$
  (\texttt{hoeffding\_two\_sample\_exponent}), vanishing three-axis split bound
  (\texttt{split\_sample\_bound\_vanishes}), and witness exponential decay.
\item \textbf{Adversarial thresholds and Nitzan--Paroush optimality}
  (\texttt{Basic.lean} and \texttt{NewTheory.lean}): Always-wrong and
  sign-flip biconditionals (\texttt{adversarial\_threshold\_div}),
  effective-family breakdown $\delta_{\mathrm{fam}} < 1/2$ invariant to clone floods
  (\texttt{effective\_family\_adversarial\_threshold}), ranking preservation
  (\texttt{ranking\_preserved}), and weighted Cauchy--Schwarz variance optimality
  with exact equality attainment (\texttt{optimal\_judge\_weights\_cauchy\_schwarz},
  \texttt{optimal\_judge\_weights\_equality}).
\end{enumerate}

The entire Lean~4 project builds cleanly (\texttt{lake build}, 3,288~jobs) with
zero \texttt{sorry} placeholders, and \texttt{\#print axioms} checks verify
that all headline theorems depend exclusively on the standard Lean axioms
\texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}.
