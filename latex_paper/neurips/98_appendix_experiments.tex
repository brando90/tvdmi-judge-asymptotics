\subsection{Experiment~15: Signal-Deconfounded Shrinkage (\texttt{SRB-Spec}), \textsc{JudgeBench}, Partial Proxies, and Rank-$r$ Spectral Bounds}

Across \textsc{RewardBench~2} ($1,753$ prompts), \textsc{JudgeBench} ($33$ official judges $\times$ $350$ pairs), \textsc{SummEval}, and \textsc{MT-Bench}, \texttt{SRB-Spec} closes clean-pool gaps via residual clone balancing and tempered precision weighting while preserving robustness to $32$-clone and inverted-polarity floods, verifying the $(1-r^2)c^2 v_W$ partial-proxy law and rank-$r$ ($r=1,2,3$) spectral guarantees.

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/exp15_srb_judgebench_proxies_and_multifactor_6panel.pdf}
  \caption{\textbf{Experiment~15 empirical verification.}
  \textbf{(A--B)} On \textsc{RewardBench~2}, \texttt{SRB-Spec} eliminates vanilla \texttt{RB-Spec}'s clean-pool gap ($0.787 \to 0.831$ Auto / $0.836$ $\alpha=1$) and strengthens clone ($0.832$) and PKU polarities ($0.837$); gains extend cleanly to \textsc{MT-Bench} ($0.803$).
  \textbf{(C)} On $33$-judge \textsc{JudgeBench} ($350$ pairs), \texttt{SRB-Spec (Auto)} holds $0.697$ clean, $0.691$ under weak clones, and $0.694$ under $46\%$ inverted judges (adding length residualization, \texttt{CR-SRB-Spec}, reaches $0.714$ / $0.709$ / $0.711$; Table~\ref{tab:app-exp15-main}).
  \textbf{(D--E)} Real-LLM and $500$-rep Monte Carlo partial-proxy sweeps match Lean formula $(1-r^2)c^2v_W+\mathrm{Var}_{\mathrm{bal}}$.
  \textbf{(F)} Rank-$r$ ($r=1,2,3$) subspace leakage decays with $0$ bound violations across $10{,}500$ i.i.d.\ draws.}
  \label{fig:exp15-overview}
\end{figure}
